Kontext reportu
Report context

Parkovacie minimá — povinný počet parkovacích miest pre každú novú budovu — sú najhlbšie zakorenená autodeprivačná norma v európskom plánovaní. V rokoch 2024–2025 ich rušia historicky rýchlo.

Tento report mapuje všetky overené reformy v EÚ + UK, Nórsko, Švajčiarsko za posledných 18 mesiacov. Pre GOSPACE LABS sú reformy priamym trhovým signálom: kde sa staví menej povinných parkovacích miest, každé jedno musí byť maximálne využité.

Parking minimums — mandatory parking space counts for every new building — are the most deeply rooted car-dependency norm in European planning. In 2024–2025, they are being abolished at historically unprecedented speed.

This report maps all verified reforms in EU + UK, Norway, Switzerland over the past 18 months. For GOSPACE LABS these reforms are a direct market signal: where fewer mandatory spaces are built, every single one must be maximally utilised.

Mestá a obce si teraz môžu nastaviť vlastné parkovacie normy od nuly po maximum 2 miesta/byt. Reforma odstraňuje predchádzajúce minimum 1 miesta/byt pre celú krajinu. Cities and municipalities can now set their own parking norms from zero up to a maximum of 2 spaces/unit. The reform removes the previous state-wide minimum of 1 space/unit. Key political compromise: the 2-space maximum cap was introduced to appease conservative CSU factions. This gives developers flexibility in design, especially in transit-rich cities like Munich, Augsburg, or Nuremberg.

Oslo zrusilo parkovacie minima ako prvu velku europsku metropolu. Reforma bola motivovana potrebou znizit naklady na byvanie a znisku emisie. Ministerske rozhodnutie z maja 2025 ujasnilo, ze existujuce plany odkazujuce na stare dynamicke normy potrebuju formalne zmeny — ide o technicky detail, nie krok spat. Oslo abolished parking minimums as the first major European city. The reform was motivated by the need to reduce housing costs and cut emissions. The May 2025 Ministry ruling clarified that existing plans referencing old dynamic norms need formal amendments — a technical detail, not a reversal. Oslo demonstrates in practice that zero minimums work: cars without dedicated garages park mainly in shared underground structures.

Helsinska mestska rada vypocitala, ze povinnosti parkovania znizuju hodnotu mestskej pody o 11 milionov EUR rocne. Reformna zona zahrnuje Pasilu (hlavny obchodny hub) a celu oblast na juh od Hakamäentie. Pre zvysok mesta plati redukcia na 50% povodnych norm. Helsinki city council calculated that parking obligations reduce city land value by EUR 11 million annually. The reform zone covers Pasila (main commercial hub) and all areas south of Hakamäentie. For the rest of the city a 50% reduction of original norms applies. The reform directly affects apartment prices — each underground parking space costs a developer EUR 30,000-60,000 in Helsinki.

4 typologie europskych reforiem4 typologies of European reform

Kazde mesto si vybralo inu cestu. Tieto styri vzorce sa opakuju napriec Europou.

Every city chose a different path. These four patterns repeat across Europe.

A

Zrusenie minimaAbolition

Zakon odstranuji min. normu. Developeri sa rozhoduju sami. Najradikalnejsi typ — najvyssi dopad na trh s bytmi.

Law removes the mandatory minimum norm. Developers decide. Most radical type — highest impact on housing market.
